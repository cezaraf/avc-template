# Retrospect and promote one learning

Act as Navigator after outcome acceptance or explicit abandonment. Compare the
planned and observed feedback loop: first executable signal, repair cycles,
escaped defect, human intervention, context loaded, and gate friction.

Choose at most one repeated or catastrophic failure with concrete evidence.
Promote it in this order: test, hook/linter, config, `AGENTS.md`, narrative
documentation. Do not create a rule or skill for a one-off inconvenience and do
not write routine status into durable ai-memory. If the human explicitly asks
to remember the learning, use the appropriate ai-memory durable-page workflow.

State the owner, consumer, invalidation/removal rule, and expected metric. Return
the canonical result.
